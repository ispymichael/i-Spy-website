How we help

Whether you need clearer direction, stronger messaging, better design or ongoing creative support, i-Spy can help at the stage that makes sense for your business.

Some projects begin with diagnosis. Others already know where they’re heading and need help with the message, design or delivery.

01

Brand Diagnosis

An independent review of your brand, audience, competitors and current communications before you commit to a rebrand, website or campaign.

Useful when

Something isn’t working, or you’re about to invest in a rebrand, website or campaign and want an independent view before you commit.

What this includes

A clear view of what to protect, what to fix and what to do next.

Not sure whether you need this? The two-minute Brand Check is a quick place to start.

02

Brand Direction

A focused workshop that helps the people leading your business agree on where the brand is heading.

Useful when

Leadership or marketing teams need to align around the audience, proposition, priorities, messaging and what should happen next.

What this includes

An agreed audience, proposition, points of difference, messaging principles and priorities.

03

Proposition & Messaging

A focused process to clarify what your business does, why it matters, who it’s for and how to explain it consistently.

Useful when

Your website, sales conversations, presentations or marketing feel unclear, inconsistent or too generic.

What this includes

A clear proposition, messaging framework, audience-specific messages and practical language for your website, sales and marketing.

04

Creative Direction & Delivery

i-Spy can take an agreed direction through into identities, websites, campaigns and final artwork, or step directly into design and delivery when the direction is already clear.

Useful when

You know where the brand is heading and need direct creative execution, a higher creative standard, stronger consistency or extra capacity to take the work through to final artwork.

What this includes

Creative direction, the right specialist team, design management, quality control and practical delivery through to final artwork.

05

Brand Partnership

Ongoing brand leadership, external perspective and creative delivery, led by Michael and supported by the right specialist team.

Useful when

You need ongoing brand leadership, a senior external perspective and regular help with design, campaigns or content.

What this includes

Depending on what you need, the partnership can include strategic guidance, brand guardianship, creative direction, ongoing design, campaign and content support, creative review, quality control and practical delivery through the i-Spy specialist network.
