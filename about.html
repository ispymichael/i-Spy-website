The story behind i-Spy

Why i-Spy exists

i-Spy was created to make experienced brand and creative thinking more accessible to businesses and organisations that need it, without the cost or complexity of a traditional agency structure.

When an organisation understands and communicates its value more clearly, it can make better decisions, protect what it has built and create stronger opportunities for growth.

Stronger organisations also support the jobs, livelihoods and communities around them, which is why the work matters beyond the brand itself.

Why the name i-Spy?

i-Spy takes its name from ‘I Spy With My Little Eye’, a game most people recognise from childhood. It starts with a simple idea: two people can look at the same thing and notice something different.

Brand work often begins in the same way. By looking together, asking better questions and bringing different perspectives, businesses can see strengths, gaps and opportunities that may otherwise be missed.

That curiosity and collaboration sit at the heart of i-Spy: helping businesses see more clearly, communicate more meaningfully and make better decisions about what comes next.

Built around what your business needs.

Some businesses need help working out what the problem is. Others know where they’re heading and need stronger messaging, better design, a new website or more creative help.

You can start at Diagnose, Define or Deliver. The right starting point depends on what is getting in the way.

The approach and the team around it are built around what the brief actually needs.

You work directly with Michael throughout. He leads the thinking, creative direction and relationship, bringing in trusted designers, writers, developers, photographers and motion specialists when the work calls for them.

You get direct advice from Michael and practical creative delivery from the right team.

Built on real business experience.

Straight out of university, Michael co-ran an industrial cleaning company that developed into a national operation, delivering supermarket refits and school and council contracts across locations including Norwich, London, Dover and Birmingham.

The company combined product sales with specialist services, giving Michael practical experience of cross-selling, client relationships, operational delivery and coordinating people across multiple locations.

It taught him that a strong brand has to work alongside the realities of the business, from the service being delivered to the people, pressures and commercial decisions behind it.

What this means for you

You leave with clearer decisions, stronger briefs and a more consistent way to talk about your business. The result is work your team can use, not another strategy document that gets filed away on a server.
