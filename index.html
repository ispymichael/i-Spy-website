Who we are

i-Spy isn't an agency with a large overhead, or a freelancer working without project management or creative direction. We are the bridge in between. A collective of talented, vetted creatives, managed from brief to artwork by a Brand and Creative Manager who has helped businesses like yours for over 20 years. Together, we provide agency quality and process without the big office and payroll costs, so your budget goes into the strategic and creative thinking, the craft and process. Nothing more.

What we do

We help you find opportunities for growth through sharper insight and clearer communication. Together with your team, we diagnose your brand against your business vision, define your difference, and bring strategic direction to life creatively through your marketing and internal communications. We turn insight into real tangible impact: A brand that's focused, distinctive, and hard to ignore.

How we do it

We keep things clear, collaborative, and simple. Our team is a close-knit group of experienced, friendly, positive thinkers and problem solvers. We are customer-centric, always working with your customer's behaviours in mind to make sure creative solutions are truly meaningful and relevant to your customers’ lives. We can start with strategic thinking, or if you need a plan brought to life, we can dip in at the design stage and bring your brand to life across on and offline communications.

Our philosophy

Brand isn't a logo. It's an extremely powerful business asset. You could argue it's your biggest. When it sits at the heart of the business, it sets direction, tightens relationships, and builds trust and recognition with all audiences. Brand, when used to its potential, makes your marketing work harder and helps you build a stronger team - future sales and recruitment become easier.

About Michael

With 23 years of experience in brand and communications, my background includes working at global agencies such as Conran Design Group and Design Bridge and Partners (formerly Super Union).

I have supported household names like Vodafone, PwC and Shell, helping them connect meaningfully with their audiences through clarity, creativity, and commercial thinking.

Alongside agency work, time spent running a business early in my career gives me a direct understanding of the real challenges businesses face, and the crucial role brand and creative thinking play in driving commercial success.

This big-business experience, combined with business-owner empathy, underpins the work I do for independent businesses, bringing the same level of strategic thinking, creativity, fun and clarity to help them achieve their potential and give clients confidence in the brand decisions they make.

At i-Spy, my focus is brand and creative strategy, overseeing curated creative teams and supporting clients to deliver a smooth process and strong, effective creative solutions.
