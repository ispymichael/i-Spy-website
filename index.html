Brand consultancy and creative delivery

Define your difference. Bring it to life.

i-Spy helps business owners, directors and marketing teams uncover what makes your organisation valuable, then turn it into a stronger brand, clearer communications and distinctive creative work that supports growth.

As your business grows or changes, your brand needs to keep pace.

You may be entering a new phase, preparing to invest or finding that your brand, website or marketing no longer reflects the business clearly.

The issue is not always the design. It may be the proposition, the message, internal alignment or the way creative work is being briefed and managed.

i-Spy helps identify what matters, agree a clear direction and turn it into distinctive creative work that helps your business build recognition, confidence and momentum.

For a quick steer, try the Brand Check. For broader questions about rebrands, messaging and websites, visit the Brand FAQs.

How i-Spy works

Diagnose. Define. Deliver.

Diagnose

Review your brand, audience, market and competitors to understand what’s working, what’s unclear and where you can strengthen your value to customers, relevance in the market and difference from competitors.

Define

Turn that understanding into a clear proposition, message and direction, with a practical roadmap setting out the priorities, stages and decisions needed to move forward.

Deliver

Bring the direction to life through distinctive, effective communications, from identities and websites to campaigns, content and final artwork.

Working with i-Spy

Every business starts in a different place. You may need help working out what needs attention, setting a clearer direction or turning an existing idea into stronger creative work. i-Spy can support you at any one of those stages, or carry the work through from beginning to end.

Brand Diagnosis

An independent review before you commit to a rebrand, website or campaign, helping you protect what works, identify the real issue and invest in the right next step.

Brand Direction

A focused workshop that helps the people leading your business agree on the audience, proposition, message and priorities, so decisions are easier to make and act on.

Proposition & Messaging

A focused process to clarify what your business does, why it matters and how to explain its value consistently across your website, sales and marketing.

Creative Direction & Delivery

Take an agreed direction through into identities, websites, campaigns and final artwork, with one experienced lead managing the work from brief to delivery.

Brand Partnership

Ongoing brand leadership, external perspective and creative delivery, helping your team maintain consistency, momentum and quality.
