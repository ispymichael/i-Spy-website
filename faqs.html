FAQs

Practical answers about working with i-Spy, from project scope and process to ongoing brand support.

Working with i-Spy

What kind of projects does i-Spy work on?

i-Spy helps businesses define, sharpen and bring their brand to life. That might mean a full brand strategy and visual identity project, a website, a campaign, a pitch deck, a set of brand guidelines, or simply helping a leadership team get clearer on what the business is really trying to say.

If your business has something good but the message or design isn’t showing it clearly, i-Spy can help work out what needs attention and put it right.

Do you only work on full rebrands?

No. A full rebrand isn’t always the right answer.

Sometimes the smarter move is to clarify the strategy, tighten the message, improve consistency, or evolve the identity without throwing away useful brand equity. i-Spy will always look at what should be protected before recommending what should change.

Can we work with you on a project basis?

Yes. i-Spy works both project-by-project and on an ongoing basis.

For a project, we agree the brief, scope, fee and what will be delivered. This works well for strategy, messaging, identities, websites, campaigns, brochures, presentations and guidelines.

Ongoing partnerships can combine strategic guidance, brand guardianship and regular creative delivery.

Do you offer retainers or ongoing support?

Yes. Brand Partnership combines ongoing brand leadership, external perspective and creative delivery, led by Michael and supported by the right specialist team.

Depending on what you need, it can include strategic guidance, messaging, brand guardianship, creative direction, ongoing design, campaigns, content, creative review and quality control.

It can help your team maintain consistency, raise the standard of the work and keep activity moving through practical delivery from the i-Spy specialist network.

How do fees and invoicing work?

Fees, project stages and payment terms are agreed before work begins, so everyone knows what to expect. Projects are usually invoiced in agreed stages, which helps both parties manage cash flow and provides a clear point of confirmation that each stage has been completed and approved before the next begins.

Standard payment terms are 28 days, and new clients may be asked for a deposit before work starts. Every project is considered individually, though, and arrangements may differ where the work forms part of a wider framework, ongoing Brand Partnership or retainer.

What are the benefits of working with i-Spy?

i-Spy combines the expertise and creative range of an agency with a simpler, more direct process. You work with Michael throughout, with an experienced team built around your brief, so there are fewer layers, clearer accountability and often lower overheads than with a traditional agency.

Compared with relying on a single freelancer, you also gain broader strategic and creative capability, experienced stakeholder management and joined-up project delivery. The result is less coordination for you and a more convenient way to get the right work done.

How does i-Spy bring in specialist expertise?

Because the team is built around the brief rather than a fixed studio structure, i-Spy can select the experience and skills that suit the work.

Some projects need one or two experienced specialists. Others need a broader creative team. Michael remains responsible for the thinking, relationship, creative direction and quality throughout.

This gives you direct senior involvement and a team selected around the needs of the work.

What is it like to work with i-Spy?

Working with i-Spy should feel friendly, clear and well managed.

The process is structured without becoming heavy, collaborative without creating unnecessary meetings, and honest without becoming difficult. Michael keeps communication straightforward, anticipates what is needed and manages the detail efficiently, with empathy for the pressures on your time, team and budget.

The aim is to make the work better while making your life easier: reliable support, clear next steps and a committed partner who keeps things moving.

Where are you based?

i-Spy is based in Norwich and works remotely with clients across the UK.

Michael is often in London and can travel for workshops, client meetings, presentations and key project moments. Most work can be handled efficiently online, but there are times when getting people in a room is still the better answer.

How does the process work?

Diagnose, Define and Deliver describe the three ways i-Spy can help, but you can enter at any point.

Some clients need help understanding the problem. Others already have a clear strategy and need stronger messaging, creative direction, design or delivery.

We agree the starting point, scope, stages, fees and what will be delivered, then keep you close to the decisions as the work moves forward.

What is the difference between the Brand Check and a Brand Diagnosis?

The Brand Check is a quick, self-guided tool that helps you identify the area most likely to need attention.

A Brand Diagnosis is a deeper independent review led by Michael. It examines the business, proposition, messaging and current brand activity, then sets out what is working, what is unclear and what should happen next.

Can i-Spy help with implementation?

Yes. i-Spy can step directly into design and delivery, or take an agreed direction through to finished work.

Michael brings together the right specialists for identities, websites, campaigns, presentations, guidelines, social content, print, digital materials and final artwork.

He stays close throughout, keeping the idea intact and the work consistent.
