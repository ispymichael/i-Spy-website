Information i-Spy may receive

The Brand Check

The i-Spy Brand Check runs within your browser.

Your answers and result are not sent to or stored by i-Spy. If you choose to email Michael about your result, the information you include will be handled in the same way as any other enquiry.

Sharing information

Information is kept only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including relevant contractual, legal, accounting and business-record requirements.

The i-Spy website does not currently use advertising, profiling or website-analytics cookies.

If this changes, this notice and any required consent arrangements will be updated before the technology is introduced.
